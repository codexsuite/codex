# Welcome to the Fallen World {.unnumbered}

## The Gods Have Fallen

Long ago the gods walked among mortals. They fell, and the world fell with them. This sample shows the
template's look: two columns, a drop cap on the first paragraph of each chapter, and the boxed blocks.

::: readaloud
The wind howls across the broken plain. Somewhere ahead, a bell is ringing.
:::

::: {.sidebar title="Playing a Fallen Cleric"}
Clerics in the Fallen World pray to gods who no longer answer. Their power comes from memory.
:::
